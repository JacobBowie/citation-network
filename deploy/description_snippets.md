## Long — for blog post intro, conference summary (≤120 words)

> In 1975, Eric Banister sketched a two-stock model — Fitness and Fatigue —
> that turned every training session into a pulse of stimulus and every
> performance prediction into the difference of two convolutions. The math
> fit on a napkin. Fifty years later, that napkin underwrites everything
> from elite-endurance taper planning to the Bayesian hierarchical models I
> use in my dissertation. *The Banister Constellation* is the family tree.
> 175 papers, 524 citation edges, a 14-paper Banister lineage chain reached
> through `parent_doi` annotations — plus an extension layer auto-classified
> by which trunk papers it links to most. The sparsity itself is the story.
